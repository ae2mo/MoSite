Meal Planner

Favorite meals, weekly plan, and a shopping list

Weekly Meal Plan

Assign a meal to Breakfast, Lunch and/or Dinner for each day. Leave a slot as "—" if you don't need one. Pick 1×, 2× or 3× next to a meal to make that many batches of the recipe — the shopping list multiplies its ingredients to match. Use "+ Add side dish" under a slot to add sides (recipes ticked "Side dish" on the Meals tab are listed first). Switching weeks keeps every week's plan separate.

Find by Ingredient

Narrow the list to meals containing specific ingredients. Type or pick from the list — each box narrows to ingredients found in meals matching what you've already picked.

Meals

Keep a list of meals you make often, with their ingredients (name, amount, unit, and grocery category). Click a meal's name to expand it and edit, or click the star to favorite it and pin it to the top of the list. These are what you'll assign to days on the Weekly Plan tab.

Shopping List

Builds a list from every meal assigned in the week on the Weekly Plan tab, combining matching ingredients into one line. It's ordered for a walk through the store: in along the outside wall through Produce, Meat and Dairy to the back, then back to the front through the center aisles (frozen, breakfast, baking & spices, canned, pasta & rice, condiments, snacks, drinks, bread). Checked items and anything you add by hand stay saved for this week.

Import a Recipe

Paste a recipe URL and click Fetch to pull its ingredients automatically. Works best with TheMealDB — paste the URL of a specific meal page (looks like themealdb.com/meal/52772-some-recipe-name), not a search or browse page. Also works on other sites that publish structured recipe data, and on RecipeDB (cosylab.iiitd.edu.in/recipedb, less reliable). If a URL doesn't work, or you'd rather not use one, just paste the ingredient list yourself below. Either way, review and fix anything before adding it to your Favorite Meals.

Amounts below are for the whole recipe — they'll be divided by 1 and saved per serving, matching how your other meals work.
