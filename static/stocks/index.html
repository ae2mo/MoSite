The Strategy

This page only looks at once-a-day price data and only trades when you click a button — there's no background process watching the market minute to minute, so literal same-day "day trading" isn't something this can honestly do. What it runs instead is a short-term swing strategy, usually holding a position days to a few weeks:

Entry — only scores candidates whose short-term trend is intact (price above its 20-day average, which is above its 50-day average), with positive but not overextended momentum, RSI in a "room to run" zone, rising volume, and real relative strength vs. the S&P 500 (it has to be leading the market, not just riding it up).

Sizing — each position is sized by its own volatility (ATR) and a fixed % of account equity risked to its stop, capped at a max % per position — not a flat equal-dollar split, so a calm setup gets a fuller position than a choppy one.

Exit — a hard stop-loss, a trailing stop once a position is up enough to protect gains, a time-stop if a setup stalls out, and a trend-break fallback.

Market filter — new buys pause while the S&P 500 itself is below its 200-day average, since even good stock-picking tends to lose money fighting a broad downtrend.

Auto mode — with the AUTO switch on, the server runs this whole routine at the top of every hour while the US market is open: refresh account data, check sell signals, sell every flagged position, scan, then buy the top picks (the market filter above still applies). It runs on the server, so it works even with this page closed, as long as the site is running. In Live mode this places real orders without a confirmation click, so use it on Paper first.

None of this predicts or guarantees a gain — it's a heuristic, rules-based ranking and risk-management tool for experimentation, not financial advice, and past performance never guarantees future results.
